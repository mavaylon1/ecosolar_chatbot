# EcoSolar USA — Company Information

## Who we are and what we do
EcoSolar USA was established in May 2015. We are based in Central Southern California. Our mission is to bring customers global quality yet local service. EcoSolar USA provides complete, customized solar solutions. We deal in both residential and commercial solar.

## Why choose EcoSolar USA
We maintain loyal partnerships with our customers and solar industry ecosystem partners. At EcoSolar USA, we promote honesty, transparency, and professionalism in everything we do. Our installation teams are highly experienced professionals who get the job right the first time. We provide complimentary post-installation maintenance.

## Unique strengths
We only deliver the highest quality systems and service. We handle all of the paperwork to get your system ready for install. Our panels are 70% more efficient over their lifetime than any other panel ever produced. Our systems come with an industry-leading 25-year bumper-to-bumper warranty covering service, parts, and power output — while traditional warranty is only 10-12 years. We will work with you to customize a solar solution that meets your needs and accomplishes your energy goals. We have a multilingual culture at our office — English, Spanish, and Vietnamese are spoken. California mandates prove that solar energy truly helps the energy problem of households and businesses. Our rating is A+ on BBB (Better Business Bureau).
